# 10 — Economy & Monetization

**Status:** DRAFT FOR LOCK

## 1. Economy purpose

Support a long 5000+ level progression without creating artificial play blockers.

## 2. Soft currency

Working name: **Petals**.

Used for:
- boosters;
- later cosmetics.

## 3. No lives baseline

Player can repeatedly:
`fail → restart`

No mandatory energy timer.

## 4. Level completion rewards

Every level:
- base Petals.

Potential bonus:
- clean/no-booster clear;
- Hard/Super Hard bonus.

Exact formula is balancing work.

## 5. Early booster economy

Unlocking a booster should grant at least one free use so the player can learn it.

Example:
- L3 Extra Slot unlock → free Extra Slot charge.
- L4 Shuffle → free charge.
- L6 Return → free charge.
- L9 Bloom Burst → free charge.

## 6. Milestone rewards

Use progression milestones to distribute:
- Petals;
- booster bundles;
- cosmetics;
- profile frames;
- feature unlocks.

No Garden Area completion rewards.

## 7. Rewarded ads

Appropriate:
- Jam rescue;
- free booster;
- extra win reward;
- optional daily reward.

User initiated.

## 8. Interstitials

Rules:
- never during puzzle;
- never immediately after fail;
- avoid first onboarding levels;
- only at natural post-win transitions;
- frequency capped by time + level count.

## 9. Remove Ads

Simple IAP:
- removes forced interstitials;
- does not remove optional rewarded ads.

## 10. Store

Simple launch Store:
- Petals;
- boosters;
- Remove Ads;
- optional starter pack.

Cosmetics may join after Wardrobe unlock.

## 11. Long-run economy

Player at Level 2000 should still:
- earn currency;
- spend boosters occasionally;
- receive milestone bundles;
- care about cosmetics/prestige.

Avoid runaway price inflation tied directly to level number.

## 12. Fairness

Boosters and ads are convenience/recovery.

No level may require spending.
