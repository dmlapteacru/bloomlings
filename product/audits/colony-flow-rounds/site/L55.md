## Механики

- Padlocks in the tray (keys on the board): a padlock tile (red, green, yellow, blue) takes its own place in a column like a capsule but cannot be tapped and blocks the column while on top. The board holds small keys of the same colours (red and green on the right of the cake, blue on the left, yellow somewhere). When the ants clear the cell holding a key, the key flies to the padlock of its colour and the padlock vanishes, the column shifting up (no tap).
- Red padlock reached the top of c2 at 67.0 and opened at 93.5; yellow padlock top of c1 at 133.0, opened 158.0; blue top of c1 at 161.0, opened ~161.5; green padlock was already green (open) when first seen at 78.5 and vanished on reaching the top of c4 at ~80.5.
- Hidden capsules (?) in c1, c2, c4; one diagonal link pair (c2 brown 30 - c3 dark 35).
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
