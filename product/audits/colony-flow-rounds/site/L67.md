## Механики

- Board counters: two '100' labels at the left and right edges of the picture's middle rows (same number on both). They count down together as the ants clear cells (98 at ~6 s, 77, 39, 1 at ~15 s) and disappear at about 16 s, i.e. after the first 100 cleared cells. What exactly they guarded (a gate, covered cells) is not visible in the tray view.
- Links: three pairs; links can span columns diagonally across rows (c1 row 5 to c2 row 5 appears as a diagonal bar while the rows differ; c3 white 14 to c4 red 36 showed as a long diagonal bar for ~50 s).
- Waiting slots compact to the left when one empties; the tray re-centres when a column empties (column identity tracked by contents).
- Hidden capsules (?) in c2 (2), c3, c4.
