## Механики

- Padlocks (key/lock): two padlock items sit in the columns like capsules but have no colour or count and are never tapped: a yellow padlock at c2 row 2 (between L22 and L20) and a cyan/light-blue padlock at c3 row 8 (between the first and second D15). Each simply disappears when its key on the board is collected (yellow at 35.75, cyan at 101.5), and the column moves up. The cover shows a yellow key in the grass under the cat and a blue key in the sky above the cat's ear.
- Links: pair a = c1 Y15 (row 6) + c4 G20 (row 6), shown as one long horizontal bar (yellow half, green half) passing behind c2 and c3 once both were on top (21.0); tapped at 82.0. Pair b = c3 D20 (row 5) + c2 G22 (row 5): a diagonal dark/green line while G22 was lower, horizontal once both on top (38.0); tapped at 43.75.
- Board box (cover only): a wooden crate with the number 43 at the bottom of the board; not visible in the tray strip, so when it opened is unknown.
- Columns re-centre when one empties (76.25, 114.5).
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
