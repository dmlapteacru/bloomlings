## Механики

- Links: two pairs. Pair a = c1 R15 (row 5) + c2 Y40 (row 5): drawn as a diagonal red/yellow line while one was lower, a horizontal bar when both were on top; one tap at 81.0 sent both to slots, which stayed joined until both were done. Pair b = c3 Y40 (row 6) + c4 B30 (row 6), same behaviour, tapped at 154.75.
- Board (cover): grey '?' hidden tiles on the house's two pillars; no boxes, no locks or keys seen in the tray.
- When a column empties the remaining columns re-centre in the tray (seen at 129.0 and 136.5).
