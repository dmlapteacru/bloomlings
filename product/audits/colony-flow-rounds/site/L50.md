## Механики

- Vertical links: two capsules in one column joined by a short bar (white 38 + pink 4 in c2, light yellow 40 + light yellow 7 in c3, light yellow 40 + red 32 in c1); one tap takes both, they hold two neighbouring slots joined by a bar.
- Padlock capsules (keys and locks): a coloured padlock occupies a place in a column (yellow in c1 under light yellow 40, green in c4 under dark 38, cyan in c2 under dark 40). It cannot be tapped; it blocks the column until the matching coloured key on the board (cover: green key at the left headphone, cyan key at the right headphone, yellow key in the lower left) is reached by the Bloomlings, then it opens and disappears without a tap. The padlock is not a capsule token here; the capsule right under it is flagged !lock. Each opened a few seconds to ~18 s after reaching the top of its column.
- Columns recentre when one empties (4 -> 3 columns at 161.5 s); column names keep their original identity.
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
