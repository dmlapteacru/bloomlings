## Механики

- Links: horizontal bars (row 1 dark pairs), vertical bar in a column (c2 pink 20 + yellow 40), diagonal bars across rows and columns that become horizontal when both members reach row 1 (c1 brown 44 / c2 yellow 30; c3 blue 30 / c4 green 40; c2 blue 35 / c3 yellow 40 spanning 3 rows; c1 white 34 / c2 light yellow 20; c1 peach 40 / c2 dark 70). Each bar is coloured half in each member's colour.
- Slots compact to the left when one frees up.
- No hidden capsules, locks or keys seen.
