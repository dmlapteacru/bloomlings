## Механики

- Padlocks in the tray: a coloured padlock tile (yellow in c2 row 2, green in c4 row 5, cyan in c1 row 5) stands in a column like a capsule and cannot be tapped; it blocks the column. The board has matching keys (small key icons on the picture: yellow at right, cyan/blue at left, green lower left). When a pod's Bloomlings reach a key, they carry it to the hole under the board, the pod in the slot shows a key icon and the padlock of that colour vanishes; the capsules below rise.
- When a column empties, the tray recentres its remaining columns (column names here stay the original ones).
- No links and no hidden capsules in this level.
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
