## Механики

- Links only: vertical pairs inside one column (c1 rows 1-2, 3-4, 6-7; c2 rows 1-2; c3 rows 1-2, 3-4; c4 rows 3-4), both go to two neighbouring places joined by a bar and keep both places until both are done.
- One diagonal link across columns: c2 red 10 (row 6) with c3 green 40 (row 7, last of c3); drawn as a long bar from c3 down-left into c2 while c2 still had capsules above; tapped as one group once both were on top (134.75).
- No hidden '?' capsules, no locks/keys, no board boxes seen.
- When two columns emptied the tray re-centred its remaining columns (134.75).
