## Механики

- Vertical links only: 13 pairs, each a capsule joined by a short stem to the one right under it in the same column; one tap takes both into two neighbouring slots joined by a bar. Most pairs pair a grey 50 with a coloured capsule.
- Padlocks with keys: a yellow padlock in c1 (between the third capsule, grey 50, and dark purple 46) and a cyan padlock in c4 (between hot pink 20 and grey 50). A padlock fills a place in its column, cannot be tapped and blocks the column until its key is collected on the board; the cover shows a cyan key in the right part of the balloon dog. The cyan padlock opened immediately on reaching the top (key already collected); the yellow one waited ~97 s on top (no yellow key visible on the cover, so its key may be hidden under tiles or behind the watermark).
- Board box: the cover shows a wooden crate with a big number 230 at the lower left of the board. It is not visible in the tray crops; what opens it (probably 230 cleared tiles) was not checked.
- Many grey/white 50s (27 S capsules): grey is the dominant background-like colour and the main slot pressure.
- Columns recentre when one empties (5 -> 4 at 462.5, 3 at 509.5, 2 at 618.25).
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
