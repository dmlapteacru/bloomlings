## Механики

- Boxes on the board (see cover and frames): a cyan crate with a number (397), a wooden box (500) and a green crate (48), each covering a block of tiles. The wooden box counted down with every tile cleared (500 -> 408 at 20 s -> 298 -> 200 -> 140 at 140 s, gone by ~200 s). The cyan crate counted down only with cyan tiles (397 -> 305 -> 195 -> 97 -> 40, unchanged from 140 to 200 s while no cyan was cleared, gone by ~250 s). The green crate stayed at 48 until the green pair was tapped (252 s), gone by ~300 s. When a box opens, the tiles under it become reachable (the red, crimson and green pods waiting in the slots then finish).
- Links: all vertical pairs inside one column (two capsules on top of each other joined by a bar coloured half and half); a tap takes both, both stay in their slots until both are done.
- When a column empties, the tray recentres its remaining columns (names here stay the original ones).
- No hidden capsules, locks or keys.
