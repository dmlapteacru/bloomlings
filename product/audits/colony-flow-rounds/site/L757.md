## Механики

- Vertical links: two or three capsules stacked in one column joined by a bar leave together with one tap (pairs p, b, c, d, e, g, h, i, j, q; triples r and k). The group keeps all its waiting places until every member is done.
- Group f mixes a vertical link (c3 light pink 10 over white 15) with a long diagonal link from that white 15 to c5 white 15 (line drawn across c4); tapped when all three were on top.
- Padlocks: a gold padlock (c2) and a cyan padlock (c4) sit in the columns as blocking items with no number; they cannot be tapped and block the capsules below. The board has a gold key and a cyan key icon; when the ants reach a key's cell the padlock of that colour vanishes and the column moves up (cyan ~112.5 s, gold ~120.5 s).
- Long stalls: the yellow 10 (pair e) and the triple r held slots for a long time because their cells were not reachable yet; the player waited ~65 s (152-216) with no tap.
- When a column empties the tray recentres. No hidden '?' capsules. Boosters not used.
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
