## Механики

- Vertical links dominate: almost every capsule is in a 2-capsule vertical pair (bar in the colour of one member) that leaves with one tap and keeps 2 waiting places until both are done.
- Two long diagonal links between neighbouring columns across 2-3 rows: c2 white 30 (row 8) with c3 white 30 (row 11); c1 white 30 (row 11) with c2 white 25 (row 11). The line points from the top capsule to a lower capsule of the neighbour; the pair becomes horizontal once both are on top.
- Long stalls of linked pairs (lime 25 held ~180 s, pink 10 ~40 s) because their board cells were not reachable yet: the main source of jam pressure.
- No hidden '?' capsules, no locks/keys, no board boxes seen. Boosters not used.
- When a column empties the tray recentres.
