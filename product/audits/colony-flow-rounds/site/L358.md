## Механики

- Board boxes: two wooden crates on the board, each with a big counter and a coloured gem: left '100' with an orange gem, right '50' with a pink gem. The counter goes down as cells of the gem's colour are cleared by the Bloomlings (orange box 100 -> 92 at 20 s -> 25 at 45 s -> 2 at 58 s -> opened by 66 s while orange 45/30/40 worked; pink box stayed 50 until the only pink capsule K50 was tapped at 67.75 s, then 49 -> 34 at 80 s -> opened ~95 s). The crate covers board cells that are cleared after it opens.
- Small counters '100' at three spots on the board (a small dark/brick block or opening under each: left of the body, right of the body, near the hat) also counted down (left 100 -> 38 at 45 s -> 22 at 58 s; right 100 -> 34 -> 18 -> gone by 58 s; hat one 52 at 20 s) - exact role unclear (seen only on the board, nothing in the tray).
- No hidden capsules, no links, no locks/keys. Columns re-centre as they empty (c3 at 41.5 s, c4 at 63.75 s).
