## Механики

- Padlock tiles in the tray (not capsules): a coloured padlock tile occupies a place in a column and blocks the capsules below it; it is never tapped and never goes to a waiting place. Its key is a small key icon of the same colour lying on the board picture; when the Bloomlings clear the cells around/at the key, the key flies to the padlock and the padlock vanishes. Yellow padlock: in c4 between row 4 (pink-red 35) and row 5 (cyan 40), on top of c4 from 96.75 until 140.4 (yellow key freed at about 140.0, after the 600 box was almost done). Cyan padlock: in c3 between row 4 (pink-red 35) and row 5 (black 45); reached the top at 158.5 and opened at 159.0 (cyan key freed then).
- Board box '600' (chocolate bar block, top right of the picture): its number counts down as the Bloomlings clear cells (600 at start, 465 at 20 s, 328 at 45 s, 277 at 80 s, 122 at 120 s, 22 at 139 s, 10 at 140.5 s) and it is gone by 145 s, uncovering the cells beneath it.
- Small board box '20' (brown, right edge of the picture, middle): visible at the start, shows a small icon by 80 s, gone by 120 s.
- Links: vertical pairs inside one column (c1 rows 1-2, 3-4; c2 rows 1-2, 3-4; c3 rows 3-4; c4 rows 3-4), horizontal pairs between c3 and c4 (rows 1 and 2), and two cross-column links c2 yellow 40 (rows 6, 7) with c3 black 45 / 47 (rows 5, 6), drawn as diagonal bars while their rows differed.
- No hidden '?' capsules seen.
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
