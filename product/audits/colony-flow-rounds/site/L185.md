## Механики

- Hidden capsules (grey '?'): 7 in total, revealed on reaching the top or when tapped as part of a vertical pair (c3 G50, c5 L50, c3 K18, c3 G83, c5 G40, c1 N46, c4 E30).
- Links of many shapes: three in a row (a, c), an L-shaped three (b: two neighbours plus the capsule under one of them), vertical pairs in one column whose lower member is hidden (d, e, g) or visible (j), long diagonal lines spanning several rows and columns (i: c2 L45 to c1 L45 three rows lower; h: c4 O1 to c1 D50; f: c5 E20 to c4 G50; m: c1 D49 to c2 R83). A tapped group stays in joined slots until all members finish.
- Unusual count: c4 holds an orange capsule with count 1 (O1, part of link h).
- Board: two dark brown chocolate-bar blocks with counters, 900 at the left edge and 500 at the bottom, plus a small '90' marker on the bear's ear (cover picture). They are not shown in the tray. The counters drop while green work runs: the bottom 500 block was already gone at 60 s, the left one read 362 at 60 s and was gone by 200 s.
- Columns re-centre when one empties (c3 at 452.75 s). No locks/keys in the tray.
