## Механики

- Board box: a brown chocolate-bar block in the lower left of the picture shows '300'; the number counts down as the ants clear cells (241 at ~10 s, 132 at ~26 s, 57 at ~42 s) and the block is gone by ~58 s, uncovering cells underneath (a red/white cup area). Probably opens after 300 cleared cells.
- Padlock in the tray: a yellow padlock takes its own place in c1 (below dark 33, above white 25). It reached the top at 127.5 and vanished at 127.75 without a tap; a small yellow key sits on the board near the cat's right ear.
- Links: two vertical pairs in one column (c2 green 45/66, c3 green 35/50) and two cross-column pairs (c2-c3).
- Hidden capsules (?) in c1 and c2.
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
