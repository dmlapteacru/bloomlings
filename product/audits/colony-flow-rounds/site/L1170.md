## Механики

- Links: many vertical pairs inside a column (bar coloured half and half), and diagonal links across columns that become horizontal once both members are on top: c4 peach 14 / c5 peach 15 and c4 dark 14 / c5 dark 15 (rows 7-8), and two very long ones from c2 (orange 13, dark 13, rows 7-8) into c1 (green 19, dark 15, rows 7-8), visible as long bars from ~22 s while c1 still had 6 capsules above them.
- When a column empties, the tray recentres its remaining columns (column names here stay the original ones).
- No hidden capsules, locks, keys or boxes seen.
