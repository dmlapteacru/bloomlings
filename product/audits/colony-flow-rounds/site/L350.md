## Механики

- Padlocks in the tray with keys on the board: green padlock in c1 (above brown 22), light-blue padlock in c2 (above white 15), yellow padlock in c4 (above red 25). Each takes its own place in the column, cannot be tapped and blocks the column while on top. Keys of the same colours sit on the board (green at the top of the picture, light blue on the face, yellow near the bottom). When the ants clear a key's cell, the key flies to its padlock and the padlock vanishes (column shifts up, no tap). Green: on top 74.75, opened 168.5 (~94 s blocked). Blue: on top 194.75, opened ~266. Yellow: on top 211.25, opened ~211.75.
- Board chocolate block: a big brown chocolate bar in the lower left of the picture labelled 700, counting down as cells are cleared (602 at 5 s, 494, 445, 383, 366, 293, 245, 169, 141, 8 at ~230 s), gone by ~255 s, uncovering red cells under it.
- Board crate: a wooden crate at the lower right with a light-blue gem and the number 50, counting down (50 until ~55 s, 32, 26, 19, 11, 1 from ~180 s to ~230 s), then gone, uncovering red cells. It seems to count something narrower than all cells (possibly light-blue cells); not certain.
- Links: many vertical pairs inside one column (e, c, f, j, g, h, i, k), cross-column pairs (a, b) and one group of three (d: a vertical pair in c2 joined to c4's light blue 15).
- Waiting slots compact to the left; the tray re-centres as columns empty. No hidden (?) capsules were seen.
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
