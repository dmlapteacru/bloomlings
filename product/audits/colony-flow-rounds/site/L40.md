## Механики

- Links: horizontal bar between neighbours (pair x after rise, pair a/b/c), diagonal line across rows (c1 row1 green 38 to c2 row2 orange 28; c3 row2 brown 25 to c4 row2 dark 40 via diagonal after c3 top left; c1 row2 maroon 20 to c2 row3 green 40), and vertical bars within one column (c4 green 40 over brown 30; c3 green 40 over dark 40). A group is tapped only when all members are on top; a vertical pair leaves together.
- Hidden capsules: grey '?' boxes reveal colour and count when they reach row 1. A hidden capsule that is linked shows a grey link line while hidden (c2 ? to c1 row 3 green 40, revealed as dark 34).
- A linked pair occupies 2 waiting places until both finish; a finished member shows as a plain coloured blob without number.
- Board: 'capybara' picture on green, no board mechanics seen (no boxes, no ? tiles). Boosters bar shows 4 boosters x2, none used.
- When a column empties the tray recentres the remaining columns.
