## Механики

- Long-range links: capsules in different columns and rows joined by a coloured rope/stick drawn from one capsule down to the other(s). Group a is three capsules (c1 blue 35, c2 white 50, c3 cyan 50); group b yellow 75 (c2) + dark 45 (c5); group c rust 50 (c4) + blue 50 (c5). Ropes run behind the booster bar to deep capsules, so a group is visible long before it is tappable; once all members are on top they show a straight bar and one tap takes all of them into neighbouring slots joined by bars.
- Hidden capsules (grey '?'): show colour and count when they reach the top (c4 blue 45, c1 cyan 35 and blue 35, c3 cyan 50, c5 white 40 and blue 40, c2 blue 20).
- Padlocks with keys: cyan padlock in c3 (under cyan 50), green in c1 (under cyan 50), yellow in c2 (under yellow 75). A padlock fills a place in a column, cannot be tapped and blocks the column until its key (cover: yellow key on the spire top, green key at the left, cyan key at the lower right) is collected on the board; then it vanishes. Cyan and green opened within ~0.5 s of reaching the top (key already collected); yellow waited ~12 s on top.
- Columns recentre when one empties (5 -> 4 at 140.5, 3 at 179.5, 2 at 253.5).
- Notation: a padlock is the column token "#<colour>"; the time it left the top of its column is in the attempt's "unlocks".
