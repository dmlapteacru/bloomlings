## Механики

- Links: horizontal bar between neighbours (c2 D28 + c3 R8), vertical bars inside a column (c1 D30+Y8, c2 G25+P25, c3 D30+W25), and a diagonal line from c1 P32 (row 3) to c2 D30 (row 2) that became horizontal when both rose to the same row.
- Hidden capsules: grey '?' boxes (c3 row 3 -> dark 30 at 69.5, c2 row 7 -> white 32 at 104.75).
- A linked pair holds both slots until both are done: red 8 (linked to dark 28) waited from 3.0 to ~100 s.
