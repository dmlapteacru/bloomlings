## Механики

- 4-capsule link chain (group a): c1 maroon 8, c2 pale blue 40, c3 orange 27, c4 dark 4, joined by coloured bars (diagonal lines while members were lower in their columns, horizontal once on top). Tapped as one when all four were on top; it occupied 4 waiting places for ~90 s because its dark/maroon/pale blue cells were not reachable yet.
- Board counter tiles: three cells show a number (60 at the left arm tip, 80 above the head, 90 at the right arm tip). The numbers fall as tiles are cleared (right one 90 -> 83 at ~40 s -> 15 at ~50 s) and vanish at 0 (left one gone by ~15 s). Exact rule (what they count) not certain.
- No hidden capsules, locks or keys seen. Boosters x2 each, none used.
- When a column empties the tray recentres the remaining columns.
