# 13 — Unlock & Milestone Roadmap

**Launch revision 2026-10-15:** the first release contains 2500 sequential levels. Long-run guidance and milestones beyond 2500 describe future content updates, not required launch content.
**Status:** LOCKED (2026-09-29)
**Owner amendment · 2026-10-26:** the **mechanic** unlock levels below are replaced by [`17_LEVEL_GENERATION_RULES.md`](17_LEVEL_GENERATION_RULES.md) §9 for new rounds; booster, store, meta and reward unlocks stay as written.
**Owner amendment · 2026-11-01 (content unlocks):** the Wardrobe moves L40 → **L44** and the Daily Challenge L50 → **L48**;
new planned features: Album scenes L27, Photo studio L60 (proposed), Master challenges L81, the Daily streak with the
Daily Challenge at L48 and a per-player access level for each calendar event. See the amendment block at the end and
[`plans/content-unlocks-plan-2026-11-01.md`](plans/content-unlocks-plan-2026-11-01.md).

This document defines **what the player unlocks as the global level number increases**.

It is separate from puzzle difficulty.

The goals are:
- frequent novelty in the first session;
- useful retention hooks early;
- complete core feature set by roughly Level 100;
- slower, sustainable milestones after that;
- support 5000+ levels without feature overload.

---

# A. Levels 1–10 — onboarding + rapid unlocks

## Level 1 — Core Play
Unlock:
- Source Pods;
- 5 Waiting Slots;
- 2 target variants;
- basic Win / Next flow.

Purpose:
player understands tap → work → clear.

## Level 2 — Third Target Variant
Unlock:
- third active target variant.

Purpose:
establish color/type ordering.

## Level 3 — Extra Slot Booster
Unlock:
- Extra Slot booster;
- one free charge.

Purpose:
first recovery tool and first economy hook.

## Level 4 — Shuffle Booster
Unlock:
- Shuffle;
- one free charge.

Purpose:
teach Source Tray manipulation.

## Level 5 — Hard Levels
Unlock:
- `HARD` difficulty label/profile;
- slightly higher reward.

Purpose:
create difficulty rhythm.

## Level 6 — Return Booster
Unlock:
- Return;
- one free charge.

Purpose:
direct recovery from premature pod commitment.

## Level 7 — Daily Reward
Unlock:
- simple daily reward claim.

Purpose:
first lightweight retention feature.

## Level 8 — Hidden/Mystery Preview
Preferred option:
- introduce first Hidden/Mystery-style Source mechanic **only if fairness design is already ready**.

Fallback:
- delay Mystery and use first Key preview instead.

This milestone must not force a weak mechanic merely to match reference cadence.

## Level 9 — Bloom Burst Booster
Unlock:
- Bloom Burst;
- one free charge.

Purpose:
fourth core booster.

## Level 10 — Leaderboard + Super Hard
Unlock:
- Leaderboard;
- `SUPER HARD` level profile;
- profile name/avatar frame baseline if needed.

Leaderboard primary metric:
- highest completed level.

Purpose:
long-term competition becomes visible very early.

First simple Key tutorial.

If not already fully present:
- all four Bloomling families now participate regularly.

## Level 25 — Milestone Reward
Reward:
- booster bundle;
- Petals;
- visible 25-level badge/checkpoint.

No new navigation.

## Level 27 — Album Scenes (planned, not implemented; owner 2026-11-01)
Unlock:

  picture; earlier matching wins count at once;

Not a level selector and no new currency. The Collection keeps showing won pictures before L27.

# C. Levels 26–50 — Bloomlings identity expands

## Level 28 — Layered Tile
Unlock:
- depth-2 target layers.

This is a major Bloomlings-specific mechanic.

## Level 32 — 5-Variant Levels
5 active target variants can now appear routinely.

## Level 35 — Garden Gate / Heavy Blocker
Unlock:
- themed large route blocker.

## Level 44 — Wardrobe / Skins
Unlock:
- Bloomling cosmetic skins;
- simple wardrobe/store cosmetic surface;
- the catalogue's first wave (55 of the 89 current items can be chosen; later waves stay at L60/L80/L100).

This is our chosen system milestone (L40 until the owner's amendment of 2026-11-01).

It is intentionally not asserted as a copy of an unverified Colony Flow feature level. The Store's clearing styles
(Animations tab) keep their own L40.

## Level 45 — Additional Target Variant
Introduce another same-family variant into normal generator pool.

## Level 48 — Daily Challenge + Daily streak
Unlock (L50 until the owner's amendment of 2026-11-01):
- one optional daily puzzle;
- separate reward;
- does not affect main Level N;
- planned, not implemented: the Daily streak, a small bonus for consecutive daily wins (proposed every 7 wins, then a
  new cycle; length not final); a missed day only ends the current streak; and a calendar of 365 prepared daily
  puzzles (date → stable puzzle id).

## Level 50 — Milestone Reward
Milestone reward:
- cosmetic or booster bundle.

# D. Levels 51–100 — core feature set matures

## Level 60 — Fountain
Unlock:
- first environmental Bloomlings-specific mechanic.

Also at L60: the Wardrobe catalogue's second wave (69 of 89) and, proposed (planned, not implemented, not explicitly
confirmed by the owner), the **Photo studio**: a postcard of a hero in owned gear on a background from finished album
scenes and an owned frame, saved or shared by the player's own action; no second lock.

## Level 70 — 6-Variant Hard Levels
Hard/Super Hard profiles may use 6 active variants.

## Level 75 — Cosmetic/Profile Milestone
Reward:
- skin, frame or visible leaderboard badge.

## Level 80 — Locked Waiting Slot
Unlock:
- selected levels can temporarily reduce usable Buffer capacity.

Also at L80: the Wardrobe catalogue's third wave (84 of 89).

## Level 81 — Master Challenges (planned, not implemented; owner 2026-11-01)
Unlock:
- a permanent archive of 5 author-checked hard puzzles (first release), free retries, one reward and a master mark
  for the first win of each; no album, streak or purchase needed; Level N never changes.

## Level 90 — Mystery Tile or equivalent
Only ship if fairness tests pass.

Otherwise substitute another validated special-object variation.

## Level 100 — Major Milestone
Unlock/reward package:
- major cosmetic;
- large booster/currency bundle;
- new background/theme rotation;
- profile/leaderboard Level 100 badge.

By Level 100 the core product should feel fully opened.

Primary progression becomes:
- board variety;
- Hard/Super Hard cadence;
- new target variants;
- milestone rewards;
- cosmetics;
- leaderboard.

Suggested milestones:

New cosmetic set / optional target variant.

### Level 750
Prestige badge + bundle.

### Level 1000

- exclusive Bloomling skin;
- leaderboard frame;
- large reward;

- 3000
- 3500

# J. Reward cadence between named milestones

After Level 100, a provisional generic cadence can layer over the exact roadmap:

## Every ~5 levels
- chance/guarantee of small booster/currency reward;
- occasional Hard level.

## Every ~10–15 levels
- Super Hard profile.

## Every 25 levels
- milestone chest/bundle.

## Every 50 levels
- stronger reward / cosmetic progress.

## Every 100 levels
- visible milestone celebration.

The exact Hard/Super Hard cadence should be irregular enough not to feel mechanical.

---

# K. Unlock rules

1. A new system unlock must be demonstrated immediately or within 1–2 levels.
2. Unlocking a booster grants one free charge.
3. A new mechanic gets showcase → practice → combination.
4. New target variants need less tutorial than new mechanics.
5. Do not unlock two major mechanics simultaneously.
6. By ~Level 100 most systems are visible.
7. By ~Level 500 the core mechanical vocabulary is nearly complete.
8. Level 500–5000 is sustained by combinations, generation, rewards, cosmetics and leaderboard progression.
