# Contract: Round Level Presentation (both builds)

What the full playtest and the Unity client show for a round level (spec 006 FR-040–FR-049, FR-064–FR-066, FR-068). It is engine-free and
lives in the kit `client/Assets/Bloomlings/UI/Design/`, linked into the playtest. Presentation never changes rules: it
reads the timeline's state, events and courier positions.

| Element | Contract |
|---|---|
| Screen sizes (owner, 2026-10-28, FR-064) | The owner's constructor values (`tools/board-constructor`) are the constants of `RoundScreenLook`: board box `BoardWidthShare` 0.953 W × `BoardHeightShare` 0.44 H from `BoardTopShare` 0.1 H (never above the top bar's buttons and the Hard badge), `FrameShare` 0.042, `GapAfterBoardShare` 0.03 H, the places' bar `SlotsRowShare` 0.085 H as wide as the box, `BoostersRowShare` 0.075 H, pod cubes `PodCubeShare` 0.15 W with `PodGapShare` 0.02 W gaps and `ColumnGap` 0.7 cube between columns; H is the reference phone's (W × 2340 / 1080). `ScreenLayout.RoundGameplay` lays them out top down; where the safe area is shorter, everything shrinks by one factor (`RoundFit`); before the boosters unlock the pods follow the places. |
| Board box (owner, 2026-10-28) | One fixed board box for every level of a screen (`ReferenceGameplayRegions.RoundBoard`), taken from the fullest layout (a badge, the booster row), so neither the picture, the columns, the badge nor the boosters move or resize it. `RoundBoardLayout.Outer` is that box. |
| Frame and gate | The rows' outer wall is never drawn per cell: it is the game's stone frame on the fixed box's edge, always the same thickness (`BoardLayout.Rim` × `Unit`, `Unit` = the box's shorter side / `FrameUnits` = `Rim` / `FrameShare`). The gate (`entries`) is the existing entry arch set in that frame in line with its inside cell, sized by `Unit` (`BoardLayout.ArchAt`); the walkers set off from the middle of the frame there. |
| Surface (owner, 2026-10-28) | Inside the frame one solid neutral surface (`board.surface`, `UiRaster.BoardSurface`: `board.surface` with a soft `board.surface_shade` along its top and left edges), like Colony Flow's board: no grid of cells, no grass, no cell tiles under the picture. The walkway ring and the picture's background (`.`) are the surface itself. |
| Cube size (owner, 2026-10-28) | The cells inside the wall (walkway ring and picture, (w − 2) × (h − 2)) take the largest cube that fits the surface, keeping the picture's proportions, centred (`RoundBoardLayout.Cell`, `Grid`): a big matrix gets smaller cubes, a small one bigger cubes, the picture stays as large as the box allows (`BoardCubeMax` caps it, 0 = none). Water, stone walls, planks, glows, Bloom Burst's rings and the walkers all derive from that cube size. |
| Oblique cubes (owner, 2026-10-28; constitution 1.2.0 VII; FR-064) | One fixed tilted viewpoint in flat pictures, no runtime projection: a column is `Cell` wide, a row `RowPitch` = `BoardRowShare` 0.88 of it, and the nearest row's front (`BoardFrontShare` 0.38, the owner's choice) under the last row (`Grid`). A cell's box (`CellBox`) is its cube's top: the hit test (`CellAt`), the walkers (`Point`), the arches, links and bridges use it. Each cube is one picture `CubeBox` = its top and its front (`UiRaster.BoardCube`: nearly flat top a little lighter than the hue, thin light rim top and left, darker right edge, a soft bevel along the top's foot, a soft blurred light spot of low alpha at its lower right (`CubeGloss` 0.32, none on pods and places), corners `CubeCornerShare` 0.12; the front a deeper, more saturated shade, never grey; `BoardCubeFill` 0.98: the cubes touch). Drawn from the far row to the near one, a nearer cube hides the front behind it, so fronts show on the bottom edge, at steps and where a cube was eaten. A side face (`UiRaster.SideFace`, `pod.cube.side`) on the side facing away from the middle, its width linear in the column's distance from it (`RoundLook.SideStep`, five widths a side, at most `SideShare` 0.10 of a cube), shows only where that neighbour is empty; between the top's and the front's shade, the left side a little lighter. |
| Strips | A stream (`#` inside the wall) is water; a wall the level's overlay laid over the picture (`board.overlay`) is a stone wall (`board.wall`), and so is the ring cell it closes. A strip cell on the walkway ring is carried on over the surface to the frame in cell-sized pieces (`RoundBoardLayout.StripPieces`), so a strip never stops short of the frame. The stream is deep water in one tone (`water.deep`, ripples in `water.foam`) with a cream stone bank (`stone.top`, `RoundBoardLayout.BankShare` 0.18 of a cell) and a dark line (`stone.line`) on every side that meets a cube or the surface (`RoundBoardLayout.Banks`, drawn over the water and under the cubes; FR-065, the owner, 2026-10-31). A bridge is a wooden footbridge with rails (`UiRaster.FootBridge`, `board.bridge`) over its site, turned to its crossing (up and down, or left and right), `PlankReach` 0.85 of a cell onto each bank and `PlankWidth` 1.5 cells wide, over the cubes. A site the level shows from the start (`hero.choices[i].shown`, absent = shown; 17 §8 as amended on 2026-10-31) is drawn at `ShownBridgeAlpha` 0.72 until its approach opens; a site not shown stays unseen until then. A buildable site is drawn whole, breathing (`BridgeBreath` 0.04), and its glow outline pulses (the owner, 2026-10-31: "the bridge's outline could pulse a little"): every `BridgePulseSeconds` 1.1 s it swells from `BridgeRingShare` 0.1 to 0.24 of a cell round the bridge and from alpha 0.45 to 1 and back (`RoundBoardLayout.BridgePulse`, `BridgeOutline`); it takes the tap; the built bridge is drawn whole and the other sites go. |
| Cubes | The oblique cube above, filled with the material's normal colour. No icon on the cube, unless the symbols mode is on (then the material's symbol, centred on its top). |
| Clearing | Each cube plays the existing clear ("eating") animation at its `clear` event time; once eaten, the neutral surface shows and nothing else (no imprint, no pale cleared cell; owner, 2026-10-28). The clearing style's walkers follow their courier route from `depart` to `arrive`, act until `clear`, and return until `done`. Since the owner's note of 2026-10-31 (the animations must be seen; the heroes are small) the act is shown until `clear` + `ClearStyles.LiveLinger` (0.6 s; the rules give it 0.1 s), the walker the same size on its way and at its cube ("V4 без роста"), `ClearStyles.WalkerCubes`: 1.4 cubes on the biggest cubes (`BigCubesAcross` 19 across the box) to 1.6 on the smallest (`SmallCubesAcross` 46), linearly in between ("1.6 when the cubes get small, 1.4 on big ones", the same day), times `ClearStyles.SmallCubeBoost` since the owner's phone report of 2026-11-02 (L54: "on such small cubes the heroes should be about one and a half times bigger"): 1 up to `BoostFromCubesAcross` 24 cubes across, 1.5 from `BoostFullCubesAcross` 34 on (L54 shows 37), linear in between, for every style and both builds (the Store's previews unchanged; the walkers stay the materials' 2D characters, Twig's the acorn: `RoundLook.WalkerVariant`), and the walk home goes at the way out's pace, not the rules' quicker way back (`ClearStyles.LiveHomeAt`, `RoundAnimator.ShownWalkers`, `HomeUs`); a walker sets off from a standstill and slows down into the gate (`GateEaseShare` 0.3 of the leg), growing from `GateScale` 0.55 and fading in out of the arch and back into it over `GateSeconds` 0.25 (the owner, 2026-10-31: no shooting out of and flying into the gate); presentation only, the rules' times stay. |
| Walkers | The family hero of the pod's material, tinted in the material's colour, for every clearing style and in the Store previews. |
| Finished picture | The win's and the Collection's picture keeps its thin stone frame, its empty cells showing the same neutral surface (no grass). |
| Pods and places (owner, 2026-10-28, FR-064) | Each column shows exactly its first `PodRows` 3 pods, one after another with a small gap, never overlapping, and nothing for the rest (no "+N"); when the top pod leaves, the others slide up. A pod is the same oblique cube with no frame or box (`UiRaster.PodCube`, `pod.cube`, `PodChip.Cube`), a lower base (`CubeDepthShare` 0.26 of its height, the owner's choice: lower than the board's), no side face (the owner, 2026-10-31; the board's cubes keep theirs); the top row's in a white outline (`PodRingShare`); a waiting pod barely lighter (`InactiveLighten` 0.12, the owner's choice; `ReadableInactiveLighten` 0.18 the most that keeps every waiting colour CIEDE2000 10 from every other), on a flatter base (`WaitingDepthShare` = `CubeDepthShare` × `WaitingFlatten` 0.7), its number half clear. The number keeps the pods' count look of before (`GameplaySkin.PodCount` in `PodChip.CountLook`'s outline), bigger, in the middle of the top; no corner count anywhere (pods, places, flights). A hidden pod is the neutral "?" cube until revealed, as the reference game's (the owner, 2026-10-31): dark neutral grey (`RoundLook.HiddenPodColor`) with a big white "?" in the middle of its top and four small ones round it, the same in every row; a just-revealed pod glows for about 1.6 s. A place shows the pod's cube (no outline) with its number centred on the cream plate, in the places' bar; a pod waiting in its place (work left, none in reach) keeps its colour, paler with its number half clear, as the columns' waiting pods (the owner, 2026-10-31: no neutral grey), with the hourglass. |
| Hidden cells (17 §8 «Скрытые клетки», 2026-11-01; FR-066) | An unrevealed hidden cell (`RoundState.Known` false, `RoundAnimator.Veiled`) is one oblique cube picture like any other cube (`UiRaster.HiddenBoardCube`, `tile.round.hidden`, the same box, front and hairline) in the hidden pod's neutral grey (`RoundLook.HiddenPodColor`) with its white "?" marks (a big one and four small ones, `tile.mystery`), never a symbol; its side face (`UiRaster.SideFace`) in the same grey. The animator keeps it veiled until its `cellReveal` event's display time (model time plus the idle offset); then it pops into its material's cube over `RoundLook.CellRevealSeconds` 0.36 s (`CellRevealScale`: a swell to 1.2 and back; `CellRevealVeil`: the "?" fading over the first 60%). The walkers never target it before (the rules reveal a cell the moment it becomes a target). Bloom Burst's rings and taps skip a veiled cube; one it takes unseen puffs as the "?" cube. No introduction card (the owner, 2026-11-01: the player sees the "?" and the cells reveal by themselves). |
| Latch and key (17 §8, 2026-11-01; FR-068; the owner's look of 2026-11-02, "Twig hero 100%", replacing that of 2026-11-01) | A closed latch stands in the place of the pod it holds (`RoundAnimator.HeldPod`: the pod right behind it, which keeps its place): `RoundAnimator.ColumnItems` gives the latch the row of that pod (`RoundQueueItem.Holds`, the pod the next item), so the column shows one item where it holds one pod; the pods behind it are drawn as waiting pods that take no tap (`HeldBehind`). While the latch is closed the held pod is **not drawn at all** (no cube, no number; its link tubes still end in its place). In its place stands our Twig hero: the owner's modular Twig V2 frames (`HeroGearArt.Layer(Family.Twig, outfit, i)`, the meta screens' `Visuals.Hero` / `HeroGearView`) in the player's equipped Twig outfit (stock Twig when there is none, e.g. the level tester), in `RoundLook.HeroCell` of the place (the hero's 448 × 504 cell `HeroCellShare` 1.34 places tall, his feet `HeroFootShare` over the place's bottom, his head a little over its top, inside the column), on a soft contact shadow (`UiRaster.SoftOval`, `HeroShadow`). He holds in front of his belly a padlock (`UiRaster.Padlock`, `pod.round.padlock`, at `RoundLook.HeroLock`) whose body is the held pod's palette colour (`RoundLook.KeyMetal`; `KeyBrass` for a hidden pod, and for a closed latch with no pod of its own, never in valid data) with a dark keyhole in a light steel ring under a steel shackle; his hands come over its sides (the hero's layers drawn again clipped to the two discs of `RoundLook.HeroHands`: `IPainter.PushClipRound` in the playtest, a circle `Mask` in Unity). In rows 2 and 3 he takes the waiting rows' lightening (a white veil of `HeroWaitingVeil` = `RoundScreenLook.InactiveLighten` over his base layer, the padlock's body `WaitingColor`). Its key (`UiRaster.UprightKey`, `board.round.key`) is an ordinary key standing upright on its tip over its empty board cell: bow (a round ring with its hole) at the top, collar, shaft, a bit with two teeth to the left, its tip `KeyHoverCells` 0.32 cells over the cell's middle so it never touches the board, with a soft oval shadow on the cell under it (`KeyShadow`, smaller as it bobs, `KeyShadowScale`); `RoundLook.KeyLengthCells` 2.4 cells long and at least `KeyBoardShare` 0.13 of the board surface's width (`KeyLength`, `KeyBox`: `KeyBoxWidth` 0.66 × `KeyBoxHeight` 1.18 key lengths, kept on the surface), over the cubes round it and under the walkers and bridges, in the held pod's palette colour, volumetric as the cubes (a darker side face below right, a face lit from the upper left with a bevel and a shine, a dark outline, a thin light halo), bobbing (`KeyBob`) and turning round its upright axis (`KeyTurns`, `KeySpin`: a flat horizontal squash cos of the turn, never under `KeyMinWidth` 12 %, mirrored and drawn in the darker `KeyBack` on its back half, `KeySpinSeconds` 1.8 s a turn). No tag colours: only when two latches hold pods of one colour do their padlock and key carry 1–3 white pips (`RoundAnimator.LatchPips`). When its `latchOpen` plays (the animator keeps it closed until the event's display time; the latch is then its column's top and its key reached) the key leaves the board and, over the tray (the playtest's `RoundPainter.KeyFlights` / `Kit.FlyingKey`, Unity's `RoundFlightsView.ShowKeys` in screen pixels), lifts off, turns faster and flies on a gentle arc into the keyhole of the padlock in row 0, shrinking to its scale (`RoundLook.KeyFlight`, `KeyInHole`, `Keyhole`; `KeyFlightSeconds` 0.48 s, facing us as it goes in), turns a quarter in the hole (`KeyTurnSeconds`) with a click ring (`KeyClick`) and fades; the shackle springs open with a swell at `ShackleOpenAt` 0.56 s, the padlock drops and fades from `PadlockDropAt` (`PadlockOpen`) and Twig steps aside toward the nearer screen edge and fades from `HeroLeaveAt` 0.6 s (`HeroStep`, `HeroSide`), over `RoundLook.LatchOpenSeconds` 0.9 s (`RoundAnimator.LatchOpenUs` 900 000, presentation only). From the event on the pod is drawn normally behind Twig and takes taps; tapped mid-opening it leaves as usual and the overlay finishes at the place. The introduction card (`RoundIntroPicture.Latch`, `RoundIntroLatch`) shows Twig with the padlock in the pod's place and the upright key turning over its cell on a patch of the board, looping (`RoundIntroLatch.Since`, `LoopSeconds` 3 s) the flight and the opening. The level tester keeps its minimal look: a padlock glyph with its body in the key's colour on the held pod, and an upright turning key glyph in its colour over its cell. |
| Links | A tube attached to the front faces of the two linked pods (`LinkTube.BetweenCubes` on the cubes, `CubeThicknessShare` 0.3 of a cube thick since the owner's "twice as thick" of 2026-10-31), in the columns and in the places. Its half nearer each pod is that pod's colour. It is not drawn above or on top of the pods. A tap refused for a missing partner shows the reason and pulses the partner. A partner deeper than the shown rows (the owner's phone report of 2026-11-02, L54: "it says there is a link, but I can't see it", and the reviews the same day: "a straight tube, just under the other pods ... not behind the phone, to the edge of the bottom row of pods"; `RoundAnimator.LinkMarks`, rows counted as `ColumnItems` draws them): one straight tube in the shown pod's colour (`LinkTube.HiddenTube`, slot `pod.link.away`, the tube's picture and thickness, its collar at the pod, an open round end, no coupling ring) from the pod's side to the middle of the bottom edge of the partner column's last shown row, slanted when the columns differ, drawn under the pods (a pod in its way covers it), never further down; the refused tap makes it glow and pulses a ring at its end; once the partner comes into the shown rows the pod-to-pod tube replaces it. |
| Speed | A normal speed-up control (×1 / ×2). Auto starts when every column is empty and no bridge is left to choose, then shows ×2 and hides the whole HUD (top bar, buttons, columns area chrome, booster bar); the board stays. |
| Introductions | The first meeting with hidden pods, links, the bridge or the latch shows its explanation card (the latch, L61, "The lock": the padlocked pod with our acorn hero and its key in the pod's colour between cubes on a patch of the board, `RoundIntroPicture.Latch`, laid out by `RoundIntroLatch`, the playtest looping the opening; "A padlocked pod waits in its column", "It opens when the Bloomlings reach the key of its color on the board", "Clear the way to the key, and the pod and the ones behind it are free"). The second meeting (L63 for the latch) shows a short reminder card. Hidden cells (L51, L53) have no card (the owner, 2026-11-01). |
| Symbols mode | A Settings switch, off by default, saved in the player's settings. When on, every material's symbol shows on cubes, pods and places. |
| End | After the last transferred level: a "new levels soon" card. |
